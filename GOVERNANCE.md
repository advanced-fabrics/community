# Governance

The project starts with a Bootstrap Lead responsible for transparent decisions, releases and maintainer appointments. Decisions and conflicts of interest are recorded publicly.

The project transitions to a Maintainer Council when it has at least three active maintainers, maintainers from at least two independent employers, and two consecutive public releases. The transition requires an accepted AFEP. The Council uses simple-majority voting for ordinary decisions and a two-thirds vote for governance or trademark changes.

No initiating company receives a permanent veto. Maintainers act for the project and disclose employer affiliation.
